# Desert structures

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, with transparent backgrounds. Originals preserved; alpha bounds above 8% plus eight pixels of padding are cropped by AtlasTexture at load time, excluding almost invisible edge noise. All three are fitted to the existing spawn rectangles.

Saved assets: `bunker.png`, `plant.png`, `radar.png` in this directory. Runtime tint, contact shadows and dust foundations are applied by SceneryObject. Mipmaps are enabled for the small on-screen size.
